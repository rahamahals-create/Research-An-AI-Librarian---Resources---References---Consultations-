# AI LibrariansGPT resource register

Last reviewed: 2026-05-18

This register collects starting points for library teams researching AI-enabled reference, discovery, literacy, and governance services. Prefer primary sources and local policies when making production decisions.

## Library and information-science references

| --- | --- | --- |

## Local policy documents to gather

Before piloting an AI librarian assistant, collect and review:

- Privacy policy and records-retention schedule.
- Collection development policy.
- Reference service policy and staff handoff expectations.
- Accessibility policy and WCAG conformance target.
- Acceptable-use policy for public computing and licensed resources.
- Vendor procurement, security, and data-processing requirements.
- Incident response and patron complaint procedures.

## Source-selection criteria for retrieval systems

Use a source only when the team can answer these questions:

1. Who owns and maintains the source?
2. How often is it updated, and how will updates reach the assistant?
3. Is the content licensed for this use?
4. Does it contain private, sensitive, or restricted information?
5. What patron groups might be harmed if the source is incomplete or outdated?
6. What staff member or group can approve changes?

## Maintenance cadence

- Review high-risk policy and service pages monthly.
- Review database descriptions and subject guides at least quarterly.
- Re-run answer-quality tests after every model, prompt, retrieval, or source update.
- Archive retired prompts and evaluation results so service changes remain auditable.
